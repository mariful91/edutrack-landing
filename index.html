FAQ

Do we need our own servers?+

No — EduTrack runs fully hosted. You just log in from a browser on any device.

Yes, we support CSV/Excel import so your current records move over in minutes, not weeks.

Is parent data kept private?+

Every campus's data is isolated and access is role-based, so parents only ever see their own child's records.
